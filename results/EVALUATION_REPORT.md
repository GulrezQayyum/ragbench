## Recommendations

### Retrieval

Use **Hit@1, Hit@3, and MRR** to evaluate retrieval quality.

- Hit@1 measures whether a relevant document is ranked first.
- Hit@3 measures whether a relevant document appears in the top three.
- MRR measures how highly the first relevant document is ranked.

### Generation

Use **Faithfulness** and **Answer Relevancy** to evaluate the
generated answer.

- Faithfulness measures whether answer claims are supported by the
  retrieved context.
- Answer Relevancy measures whether the answer addresses the question.

### Important

This analyzer intentionally does **not** report `context_relevancy`
or `context_recall` because the current `eval.py` does not produce
those fields.
